BidPort — Online Car Auction

BidPort is Lebanon's first dedicated online car auction platform. Users download the app, browse live auctions, and buy or sell cars, motorcycles, and buses with transparent pricing and a small platform fee. The design breaks away from typical automotive aesthetics with a fresh teal-and-orange color scheme, intuitive vehicle finder, and streamlined bidding experience.
